# Pitch outline

**Problem:** Growing uncertainty about changing occupational skills, compounded by little visibility into alternative careers.

**Solution:** FutureWork AI matches transferable occupational skills and overlays Australian labour-market signals when verified mappings are available.

**IBM LinuxONE:** Python/Pandas/scikit-learn/JupyterLab container hosted on IBM LinuxONE; demonstrate actual execution. Do not claim speedups or specialised AI acceleration without benchmarks.

**Tech for Good:** Explainable recommendations support workers who may not have access to career advice.

**Differentiator:** Two views: skill compatibility vs evidence-aware opportunities; transparent mapping provenance and no-data labels.

**Limits:** Not personalised salary advice, not guaranteed hiring, group growth != exact occupation growth, pilot mappings only, not validated suitability.

**Roadmap:** External job-ad signals, more verified AU mappings, training sources and qualification checks, community testing.
